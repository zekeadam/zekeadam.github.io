Port Ellen Whisky
Client Bose Collins / Global Illumination / Proud Robinson + Partners
Role 3D Artist & Developer
HoudiniPythonOctane Render
A generative, fractal-like visualization driven by complex chemical data from Port Ellen whisky. The form evolves as the whisky matures, visually reflecting its transformation in the barrel. I developed the complete system, art direction, lighting and rendering, delivering most of the final imagery and bringing science and art together to express the spirit of smoke.
